# Political prisoners found in The Black Panther, Vol. 14 (1975-76)

Read page-by-page from the marxists.org archive of the Black Panther Party newspaper
(<https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/black-panther/index.htm>) — all 28 available issues of Volume 14 (Sept. 1975 – Apr. 1976; the archive
skips no. 8 and no. 11), roughly 1,090 pages of dense newsprint rendered to images and read
one page at a time. Each named individual below was presented by the paper as a political
prisoner or the subject of a political prosecution. Descriptions record what the paper
reported (1975-76), with a few well-established later outcomes noted.

Volume 14 is dominated by the **San Quentin 6** trial, the **Fred Hampton** civil-rights
damages trial, the **Attica Brothers** prosecutions, the **Dessie Woods–Cheryl Todd** and
**Inez Garcia / Yvonne Wanrow / JoAnne Little** self-defense cases, the **Wilmington 10**,
the AIM cases (Pine Ridge, Custer, the Loud Hawk defendants), the **Puerto Rican Nationalist**
prisoners, and **Gary Tyler** — so many figures recur across issues and are cited with their
most detailed appearance.

## Categories of people NOT added (with reasons)

- **Fallen BPP comrades killed by police or rivals** (Bobby Hutton, Bunchy Carter, John Huggins, Sylvester Bell, John Savage, Spurgeon Winters, Frank Diggs, Sam Napier, Welton Armstead, Sidney Miller, Walter 'Toure' Pope, Bruce Washington, Nathaniel Clark, and Jonathan Jackson). Martyrs, but never imprisoned — not political-prisoner cases.
- **Soledad guard-killing victims** (W.L. Nolen, Cleveland Edwards, Alvin Miller) and other inmates killed by guards (Fred Billingslea, Yusuf/Joe Jones, Tommy Dotson, George Dobbins) — custodial killings, not political prosecutions.
- **The 'Zebra murders' defendants** (Larry Green, Manuel Moore, J.C. Simon, Jessie Lee Cooks), the **SLA** (Patty Hearst, Bill & Emily Harris), and **James Earl Ray** — the paper covered these but they were random-victim killings / not sympathetic political-prisoner cases.
- **Prison-conditions letter-writers, hunger-strikers, jail-rebellion and jailhouse-lawyer retaliation cases** (the N.C. women's-prison protest group, Marion/Stateville/Menard/Attica/Rikers/Waupun figures, the Pontiac Prison Legal Program transfers) whose underlying convictions are unknown or apolitical.
- **Coerced witnesses, fugitive attorney Stephen Bingham, police-brutality victims not jailed, and purely historical/rhetorical mentions** (Angela Davis, Dave Dellinger, the L.A. CAPA defense-committee name list, etc.).
- **Griselio Torresola / David Pratt-type attackers and foreign mercenaries** (the Angola-captured American pilots, the Botswana-jailed VVV men) — perpetrators, not sympathetic prisoners.

Many recurring iconic figures (the San Quentin 6, Rubin Carter & John Artis, Ruchell Magee, Gary Tyler, Delbert Tibbs, Anna Mae Aquash, Leonard Peltier, the Loud Hawk defendants, H. Rap Brown, Bobby Seale, Lolita Lebrón, Andrés Figueroa Cordero, Pedro Albizu Campos, Wilmington 10 leaders) already entered the database via the Vol. 15–19 imports and are skipped by `prisoner:add`'s name de-dupe.
