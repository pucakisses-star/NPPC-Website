# Political prisoners found in The Black Panther, Vol. 13 (1975)

Read page-by-page from the marxists.org archive of the Black Panther Party newspaper
(<https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/black-panther/index.htm>) — all 30 issues of Volume 13 (Feb. – Sept. 1975), roughly 1,110 pages of dense
newsprint rendered to images and read one page at a time. Each named individual below was
presented by the paper as a political prisoner or the subject of a political prosecution.
Descriptions record what the paper reported in 1975.

Volume 13 is dominated by the San Quentin 6 trial, the **JoAnne Little** trial, the **Attica
Brothers** prosecutions, the Alabama **Atmore-Holman Brothers** (Inmates For Action), the
**Maryland Penitentiary Five**, the **Leavenworth Brothers**, the Omaha Two, and the Wounded
Knee/AIM cases. Because Vol. 13 immediately precedes Vol. 14, many figures recur — those
already entered via the Vol. 14-19 imports are omitted here (and would be skipped by
`prisoner:add`'s name de-dupe anyway); this file captures the cases NEW to Vol. 13.

## New group cases surfaced in Vol. 13

- **Atmore-Holman Brothers (IFA)** — six more of the nine Alabama Inmates For Action defendants not already in the database (Edward Ellis, Anthony Paradise, Lincoln Heard, Charles Beasley, Grover McCorvery, Jessie Clanzy); Gamba Mani/Oscar Johnson and Johnny Harris were already added via Vol. 14-15.
- **Maryland Penitentiary Five** — Marshall 'Eddie' Conway (the Baltimore BPP prisoner), Robert Foulks, Clifton Wiggins, Robert Austin.
- **Leavenworth Brothers** — Odell Bennett, Alf Hill, Jesse Lopez, Alfred 'Jazz' Jasper, from the 1973 Leavenworth uprising.
- **Los Tres del Barrio** — Alberto Ortiz, Rodolfo Sanchez, Juan Fernandez (Chicano activists).
- **Tarboro 3** — Bobby Ray Hines, Vernon Brown, Jesse Lee Walston (death-row rape convictions overturned).
- **Pensacola ministers** — Rev. B.J. Brooks and Rev. H.K. Matthews (extortion convictions for civil-rights protest).
- Individual additions include Charles Joe Pernasilice (Attica), Cameron Bishop, Larry James Pinkney, Scott Camil, Eddie Sanchez, Gary Lawton, Sonny Carson, Ralph Moore, Robert Steele Collier, Elaine Brown, and R.A. Cooks.

## Categories NOT added (with reasons)

- **Fallen BPP comrades killed by police or rivals** (Bobby Hutton, Bunchy Carter, John Huggins, Sylvester Bell, John Savage, Carl Hampton, Babatunde Omarwali, Sam Napier, Joe-Dell Waddell, Larry Roberson, etc.) — martyrs, not imprisoned.
- **The 'Zebra' murder defendants**, the **Kanawha Valley school-bombing** convicts (right-wing textbook protesters), and **James Earl Ray** — the paper covered these but they are not sympathetic political-prisoner cases.
- **The Houston 12** anti-war defendants (all received probation/fines with no jail time) and various minor police-harassment arrests (BPP sickle-cell solicitors, etc.).
- **Prison-conditions letter-writers, hunger-strikers, and custodial-death victims** (Waupun/Marion/McAlester/Menard/Maryland-Pen figures, the Missouri Penitentiary 9 named only in an inmate letter, etc.).
- **Coerced witnesses, fugitive attorney Stephen Bingham, and historical/rhetorical or reprinted-list mentions** (Marcus Garvey, Muhammad Ali, Tom Hayden, the 1970 Soledad strike-demands list including Chip Fitzgerald).
