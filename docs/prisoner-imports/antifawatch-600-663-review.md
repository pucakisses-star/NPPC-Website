# AntifaWatch pages 600–663 — prisoner-database review

Source lead list: `antifawatch.net/Browse/600`–`/663` (~1,280 entries). That site is a partisan
watchlist and was used **only to surface names**. Every person below was then independently
verified against court records, DOJ/U.S. Attorney press releases, or established news reporting.

**Inclusion bar:** the person actually **served jail or prison time**, or was held in
**substantial pretrial detention**, in a protest-connected case. Arrest-only, dropped/dismissed
charges, acquittals, probation-only, and fine-only outcomes were excluded.

**Why most entries were excluded:** pages 651–663 are largely the January 20, 2017 (J20)
inauguration mass arrests, where the watchlist itself notes "held but never charged, not a
single conviction"; other large blocks are Portland/Dallas mass-citation lists (cited-and-released)
and 2020 felony-rioting charges that were later dropped.
