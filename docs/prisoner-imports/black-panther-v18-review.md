(nos. 1–8, 12, 17, 26, 28, 29 — 300 pages). Descriptions record what the paper reported (1978),
with a few well-established later outcomes noted (e.g., the Wilmington Ten pardon).

## Named but NOT added (with reason)

- **Marcus Garvey**, **Angela Davis**, **Cesar Chavez**, **Muhammad Ali** — historical/retrospective references or iconic figures almost certainly already in the database (Ali was never actually imprisoned; his conviction was overturned).
- **James Earl Ray**, **Sirhan Sirhan** — the convicted King/RFK assassins, covered under conspiracy theories; not sympathetic political-prisoner cases.
- **Dale Pierre & William Andrews** (Utah 'Hi-Fi' murders), **Larry Green** (SF 'Zebra' killings), **Michael Payne**, **Gregory Teron** — ordinary murder/robbery convictions covered as death-penalty items, not political prosecutions.
- **George & Larry Stiner** — convicted of murdering the BPP's Bunchy Carter and John Huggins; not political prisoners of the movement.
- **Larry Layton** (Jonestown/Rep. Leo Ryan murders), **Charles Diggs** (congressional payroll fraud), **Mark Rudd** (probation only), **Preston Callins** (a witness jailed for refusing to testify), **Renault Robinson / William Bigby / Franklin Nash** (minor police-harassment arrests), the **ECCO/Hancock County** fraud defendants, and several letter-writers with no case stated (Harry James Snow, Judge Wade, Al Featherston, Zola Azania, Abdul Karim Mu'min).
