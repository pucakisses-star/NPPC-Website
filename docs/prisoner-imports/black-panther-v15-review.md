# Political prisoners found in The Black Panther, Vol. 15 (1976)

Read page-by-page from the marxists.org archive of the Black Panther Party newspaper
(<https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/black-panther/index.htm>) — all 30 issues of Volume 15 (Jan.–Nov. 1976), roughly 1,290 pages of dense
newsprint rendered to images and read one page at a time. Each named individual below was
presented by the paper as a political prisoner or the subject of a political prosecution.
Descriptions record what the paper reported in 1976, with a few well-established later
outcomes noted (e.g. Geronimo Pratt's and Rubin Carter's exonerations).

Volume 15 is dominated by the 16-month **San Quentin 6** trial, the **Butler–Robideau**
(Pine Ridge / Leonard Peltier) federal trial, the **Fred Hampton** civil-rights damages
trial, the **Wilmington 10**, **Gary Tyler**, and the Puerto Rican independence grand-jury
cases — so many figures recur across issues and are cited with their earliest/most detailed
appearance.

## Categories of people NOT added (with reasons)

- **Fallen BPP comrades killed by police / rivals** (e.g. Bobby Hutton, Bunchy Carter, John Huggins, Fred Bennett, Spurgeon Winters, Sam Napier, Sidney Miller, Steve Bartholomew, Robert Lawrence, Tommy Lewis, Denzil Dowell, and the long 10th-anniversary honor roll in no.27). These are martyrs but were never imprisoned — not political-prisoner cases.
- **Jonathan Jackson, James McClain, William Christmas** — killed during the Aug. 7, 1970 Marin County Courthouse armed action, not prisoners serving time (Ruchell Magee, the survivor, IS included).
- **Ordinary death-penalty cases the paper covered as anti-death-penalty causes** — Mable Glenn, Michael Jackson, Joseph Velez, Anthony Machetti, Troy Leon Gregg, Ehrlich Coker, Louis Monge, Michaiah Shobek — ordinary murders/rapes, not political prosecutions.
- **Prison-conditions letter-writers, hunger-strikers and jail-strike detainees** whose underlying convictions are unknown or apolitical (the Lucasville death-fast group, the Maryland Penitentiary solidarity-contact list, Bronx/Attica/Waupun strike figures, etc.).
- **Coerced government witnesses** (Norman Brown, Wilford Draper), **fugitive attorney Stephen Bingham** (later acquitted), and people named only in **rhetorical, historical or film-review** mentions (Angela Davis, JoAnne Little, Inez Garcia, Sacco & Vanzetti).
- **Anti-Castro Cubana-bombing suspects** (Orlando Bosch, Luis Posada, Herman Ricardo, Freddy Lugo) and **assassins** (David Pratt) — perpetrators, not political prisoners.
- **Marshall 'Eddie' Conway** appears only as a bare name in a Maryland Penitentiary contact list here, with no case described in the paper, so he is not added on this source alone despite being a well-known BPP political prisoner.
