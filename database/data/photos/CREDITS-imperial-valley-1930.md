# Imperial Valley criminal-syndicalism defendants (1930)

The portraits in `photos/imperial-valley-1930/` are cropped from a single
captioned group photograph of the Imperial Valley prisoners published in
**Labor Defender**, Vol. 5, No. 7 (July 1930), the magazine of the
International Labor Defense (New York).

Original caption:

> The Imperial Valley prisoners (Front row, left to right): Danny Roxas,
> Oscar Erickson, Braulio Orosco, Carl Sklar, Tetsuiji Hariuchi; (Second
> row, left to right): F. Funes (charges dropped), Eduardo Herrera,
> Lawrence Emery, Emilio Alonzo and Frank Spector.

Each file is named for the prisoner it depicts and was cropped from that
person's position in the group photo. The individual crops are:

- `danny-roxas.jpg` — Danny Roxas (front row, 1st)
- `oscar-erickson.jpg` — Oscar Erickson (front row, 2nd)
- `braulio-orosco.jpg` — Braulio Orosco (front row, 3rd)
- `carl-sklar.jpg` — Carl Sklar (front row, 4th)
- `tetsuji-horiuchi.jpg` — Tetsuji Horiuchi / "Hariuchi" (front row, 5th)
- `eduardo-herrera.jpg` — Eduardo Herrera (second row, 2nd)
- `frank-spector.jpg` — Frank Spector (second row, 5th)
