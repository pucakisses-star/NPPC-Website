# Outstanding prisoner photos

People requested for a portrait whose source could **not** be retrieved
automatically — almost always because the linked page renders its image with
JavaScript, sits behind a paywall/stock watermark, or resolved to a
non-portrait image (logo, group scene, illustration). Everything here still
needs either a **direct image URL** (right-click the photo → *Copy Image
Address*) or a rights determination.

Photos that *were* added this session live in `nonfree/` and are attached by
the `prisoners:attach-*` commands; see `CREDITS-nonfree.md` for provenance.
