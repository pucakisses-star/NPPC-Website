# Non-free prisoner photos (used under a fair-use / memorial rationale)

The images in `photos/nonfree/` are **copyrighted** and are **not** public-domain
or freely licensed. Unlike the Wikimedia Commons portraits credited in
`CREDITS-wikipedia.md`, these are retained at the site owner's direction and used
for the same non-commercial, educational, political-prisoner memorial purpose for
which they appear on their source pages, at low resolution. Remove any image here
if a rights-holder objects.

The attach command (`prisoners:attach-nonfree-photos`) only fills entries that
have no photo and never overwrites an existing image.
