# Homer Brooks portrait

`photos/homer-brooks.jpg` is cropped from the January 18, 1937 wedding
photograph of Emma Tenayuca and Homer Brooks — Homer is the figure on the
left; Emma has been cropped out.

- Source: Wikimedia Commons, `File:Emmamarriage.jpg`
- Original: "Emma Tenayuca and Homer Brooks' wedding portrait," San Antonio
  Light Photograph Collection, L-1662-A, UTSA Special Collections
