# CNVA / Polaris Action portraits — provenance and rights

Seven portraits attached by `database/data/fix-cnva-polaris.sh`
(batch 52), from the sources the curator's dossier identified. All
cropped to 525×700; identification anchors recorded per image.

## From David N. Rich's Walk for Peace archive
(davidnrich.com/56-65/WalkForPeace/photos/ — his own captioned scans of
the 1960–61 San Francisco-to-Moscow CNVA walk)

- **allan-hoffman.jpg** — Vandenberg Air Force Base, December 1960.
  Caption: "Left to right, visible faces: Scott Herrick, Allan Hoffman,
  Dave Rich, Ray Wisniewski, Julius Jacobs and Jerry Lehmann." Hoffman
  is the second visible face — the seated man in glasses. SMALL FACE,
  from a lunch-break group photo; kept as an identified contemporary
  image, replace if a portrait surfaces.
- **jerry-wheeler.jpg** — Monterey Peninsula, December 1960. Caption:
  "Right to left: Joe Glynn, Jerry Wheeler, Milton Mayer and Regina
  Fischer in the lead." Wheeler is second from the right — the man in
  the light jacket carrying the round CNVA "Walk for Peace" sign. The
  caption adds, contemporaneously: "Jerry Wheeler is now serving a six
  month term in prison as the result of a protest demonstration at a
  missile base" — independent corroboration of the sentence his record
  carries.

## From Gene Keyes's own CNVA photograph archive
(genekeyes.com — "All photos by or for Gene Keyes on his cheapo
camera", scanned and captioned by Keyes himself, 2009–2010)

- **eugene-keyes.jpg** — captioned simply "Gene Keyes.": his solo
  photograph at the December 1961 Urbana vigil in support of the
  Committee of 100, holding the "NEITHER RED NOR DEAD" sign — the image
  the dossier rated the highest-confidence choice.
- **edward-sanders.jpg** — "Ed Sanders and Penny Young, at NECNVA house
  in Norwich" (June 1961, Polaris Action set). Sanders is the left
  figure; the same face recurs in "Ed Sanders and Gene Keyes" from the
  same set, which pins the identification. Penny Young is cropped out.
- **ed-guerard.jpg** — "Ed Guerard and Gordon Christiansen" (March 1961
  Quincy reconnaissance set). Guerard is the left figure, confirmed
  across the set's three captions by elimination: the older man in
  glasses recurs as Christiansen. FACE-AVERTED — he is looking down,
  writing. Kept on the Berthe Arnold rule (batch 31): an identified
  contemporary image beats an unidentified clear one.
- **bill-henry.jpg** — "Bill Henry, Beverly Sterner" from the 2010 CNVA
  reunion set. Henry is the seated man; Sterner is cropped to the edge.
  The dossier also identifies a contemporary photograph of Henry
  swimming toward the Ethan Allen at the lower left of page 6 of the
  November 28, 1960 National Guardian — historically stronger, not yet
  located as a scan.
- **marjorie-swann.jpg** — "Marj Swann, Gene Keyes, DeCourcy Squire"
  from the 2010 reunion set, left to right; Swann is the left figure in
  the purple beret. Swarthmore's Marjorie and Robert Swann Papers hold
  the preferable period photograph (August 1959, at the Mead missile
  site); this is the accessible one.

## Pursued and NOT obtained

- **Donald Martin** — a photograph definitely existed: the November 23,
  1960 New York Times reportedly ran an AP photo of Martin on the Ethan
  Allen's hull. No open scan located. Targets: that Times issue, and
  the Swarthmore College Peace Collection's CNVA photograph files.
- **Richard Zink** — his Dignity Memorial obituary (the dossier's
  identified portrait) is Cloudflare-blocked from this environment and
  not in the Wayback Machine:
  <https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/huntingdon-valley-pa/richard-zink-5592421>
  Needs an ordinary browser; drop the portrait in as
  `database/data/photos/richard-zink.jpg` and re-run. The National
  Guardian of November 28, 1960 (page 6) also pictures him, captioned
  "Richard Zink sighted sub, rowed toward same."

Both absentees are listed in the attach loop, so completing them is
