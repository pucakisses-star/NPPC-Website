# Woodlawn sedition case — portraits

Three portraits cropped from one page of an International Labor Defense
pamphlet, `SEDITION — To Protest Against Unemployment`, page 16. The page
carries the three men one above the other with a caption under each, and
each crop is taken from the position its own caption sits beneath.

Source copy: `redpgh.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/sedition1.png`
(Red Pittsburgh), a 541x755 grayscale scan of the printed page.

**Treatment.** Cropped to the printed photograph alone, inside the frame
rules, dropping the captions and the page furniture. Converted from
grayscale to RGB and saved as JPEG. Kept at the resolution of the scan --
152x184, 212x203 and 213x203 -- and deliberately not upscaled: the halftone
screen of the original printing is visible at this size and enlarging it
would add dots, not detail.

**Rights.** An International Labor Defense pamphlet of the early 1930s.
Almost certainly public domain by age and by non-renewal, which is why
these sit here rather than in `nonfree/`. Two caveats worth recording
rather than glossing: the pamphlet carries no date on this page, so the
exact publication year is not established here; and the provenance of this
particular scan is a WordPress upload rather than a library copy. If a
rights-holder objects, remove them.
