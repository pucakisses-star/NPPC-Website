# Jericho Movement prisoner photos

The images in `photos/jericho/` are the prisoner portraits published by the
**National Jericho Movement** on its prisoner pages — the "Ancestors"
(https://www.thejerichomovement.com/prisoners/ancestors), "Freed Political
Prisoners" (https://www.thejerichomovement.com/prisoners/freed-political-prisoners)
and current prisoners (https://www.thejerichomovement.com/prisoners) lists —
downloaded from the individual `/profile/<name>` pages (`.../person_photos/...`).

They are used here for the same non-commercial political-prisoner memorial and
solidarity purpose for which the Jericho Movement distributes them. They are
attached only to prisoner entries that have no photo (the attach command never
overwrites an existing image).

Source: The National Jericho Movement — thejerichomovement.com
