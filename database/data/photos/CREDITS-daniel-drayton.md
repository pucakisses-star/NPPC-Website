# Photo credit — Daniel Drayton

`daniel-drayton.jpg` — Captain Daniel Drayton (1802 – July 1, 1857), who
commanded the schooner Pearl in the April 1848 mass escape attempt from
Washington, D.C. and was imprisoned for it.

Source: a mid-19th-century engraved portrait of Drayton, as digitized by the
House Divided Project, Dickinson College
(`hd.housedivided.dickinson.edu/.../HD_draytonD.jpg`). The engraving dates to
the 1850s (it accompanied his 1855 memoir) and is in the public domain by age.
