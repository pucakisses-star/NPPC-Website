# Photo credits — Civil War governors held without charge in 1865

Both men were cellmates at the Old Capitol military-prison complex in
Washington after the Confederate surrender, and both portraits are
nineteenth-century photographs in the **public domain** — no licence
condition attaches, but the provenance is recorded here anyway so the
sourcing can be checked.

## zebulon-vance.jpg

- **Subject:** Zebulon Baird Vance, wartime governor of North Carolina
- **Photographer:** Mathew Benjamin Brady / Levin Corbin Handy
- **Date:** between 1870 and 1880
- **Collection:** Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division,
  Brady-Handy Photograph Collection
- **Via:** Wikimedia Commons,
  `File:Zebulon Baird Vance - Brady-Handy.jpg`
- **Status:** Public domain
- **Source resolution:** 2952 × 3488, greyscale
- **Processing:** cropped from the seated three-quarter original to a
  head-and-shoulders portrait and resized to 524 × 700. No retouching.

## john-letcher.jpg

- **Subject:** John Letcher, wartime governor of Virginia
- **Photographer:** Mathew Benjamin Brady
- **Date:** circa 1860–1865
- **Collection:** U.S. National Archives and Records Administration,
  NARA identifier 528418
- **Via:** Wikimedia Commons,
  `File:Hon. John Letcher, Va - NARA - 528418.jpg`
- **Status:** Public domain
- **Source resolution:** 2319 × 3000, greyscale
- **Processing:** the original is a scan of a glass plate, complete with
  a light mount border and chipping at the edges and corners. The crop
  is taken well inside the plate so that the border and the damaged
  corners are excluded, then resized to 525 × 700.
- **Left as it is:** a bright emulsion blemish remains on his shoulder.
  That is a defect in the plate itself, not in the scan, and it does not
  obscure the subject. Retouching a historical negative is a different
  act from cropping one, so it stands.

A higher-cropped Library of Congress version of the Letcher portrait
also exists on Commons (`... (3x4 cropped).jpg`) but is only 508 × 668,
so the NARA plate was used instead for the resolution.
