# Camden 28 portraits — provenance and rights

Six portraits attached by `database/data/attach-camden-28-photos.sh`
(batch 51), pursued on the curator's instruction from the sources the
Camden 28 research report identified. All are modern photographs of
named, publicly identified defendants, used with attribution recorded
here; none is public domain, which follows the site's existing practice
for current-era records. All cropped to 525×700.

## father-michael-doyle.jpg — Msgr. Michael Doyle

- **Source:** Catholic Star Herald (Diocese of Camden), obituary
  "Msgr. Michael Doyle, 'The Heart of Camden,' dies" —
  <https://catholicstarherald.org/msgr-michael-doyle-the-heart-of-camden-dies/>
- **What it is:** his diocesan portrait — clerical collar, the image his
  own diocese chose to announce his death.
- **Why this is him:** it accompanies his named obituary from the diocese
  he served for six decades.

## edward-mcgowan.jpg — Edward John McGowan

- **Source:** Lansing Funeral Home (Lansing, NY), obituary —
  <https://www.lansingfuneralhome.com/obituaries/edward-mcgowan>
- **What it is:** his obituary photograph, holding his fiddle — the
  obituary calls him "a writer, Catholic peace activist, and renowned
  musician".
- **Why this is him:** the obituary confirms his birth May 7, 1935, his
  death September 28, 2020, and his August 22, 1971 Camden arrest "with
  27 others" — the same facts batch 50 recorded.

## john-swinglish.jpg — John Robert Swinglish

- **Source:** HoCoMD memorial post, "John Swinglish, a member of the
  Camden 28, has died" (May 12, 2017) —
  <https://hocomd.cc/2017/05/12/john-swinglish-a-member-of-the-camden-28-has-died/>
- **What it is:** the later-life photograph from the memorial's
  then-and-now pairing. THE OTHER HALF OF THAT PAIRING IS THE FILM
  POSTER: the man in handcuffs flashing a peace sign on the documentary's
  poster is Swinglish's own arrest photograph, which is why the dossier
  lists "an arrest photograph showing him giving a peace sign". The
  poster version carries heavy graphic overlay, so the clean modern
  photograph is used for the portrait.

## keith-forsyth.jpg — Keith Forsyth

- **Source:** WHYY, "How to break into the FBI: 50 years later, Media
  burglars get local honors" (photograph by Emma Lee/WHYY, August 2021) —
  <https://whyy.org/articles/how-to-break-into-the-fbi-50-years-later-media-burglars-get-local-honors/>
- **What it is:** Forsyth at the site of the Media, Pennsylvania FBI
  office his group burgled in March 1971, on the fiftieth anniversary.
- **Why this is him:** the caption names both figures — "Bonnie Raines
  and Keith Forsyth… standing at the site where a historical marker will
  commemorate the break-in." He is the right-hand figure; the crop takes
  him.
- **Full frame:** 2100×2100-class original (2100×1400) preserved in the
  session record; Bonnie Raines is cropped out, not erased — the source
  URL has both.

## anne-dunham.jpg — Anne Grant Dunham

- **Source:** South Dakota Public Broadcasting, "The Camden 28: Standing
  Against The Vietnam War" (In the Moment, September 6, 2017) —
  <https://www.sdpb.org/blogs/margins/the-camden-28-standing-against-the-vietnam-war/>
- **What it is:** the studio photograph from the 2017 radio interview
  the dossier cites — Frank Pommersheim on the left, Dunham on the
  right. The crop takes her.
- **Why this is her:** the article names both guests, and Pommersheim's
  face matches his University of South Dakota faculty portrait already
  on his record, which pins which figure is which.

## kathleen-ridolfi.jpg — Kathleen "Cookie" Ridolfi

- **Source:** Santa Clara Law, "Linda Starr and Cookie Ridolfi"
  (law.scu.edu/wp-content/uploads/, retrieved via the Wayback Machine —
  the live SCU site now 302s its old faculty pages to an error page).
- **What it is:** a Northern California Innocence Project photograph of
  its two founders; the filename names both women.
- **Why the RIGHT figure is Ridolfi:** her confirmed SCU faculty
  headshot (`wpid-ridolfi-kathleen26.jpg`, 130×172 — too small to use as
  a portrait) shows the same face: short white hair, broad smile. The
  1346×1536 pair photograph matches it and is used instead.

## Pursued and NOT obtained

- **Robert Williamson** — his May 2026 Neptune Society (Albuquerque)
  obituary is behind a Cloudflare block from this environment and is not
  in the Wayback Machine. Needs an ordinary browser. Drop the portrait
  in as `database/data/photos/robert-williamson.jpg` and re-run the
  attach script; he is already listed in it.
- **Margaret Inness** — her Baker McCullough (Savannah) obituary carries
  NO portrait, only stock bird art for her Audubon work, despite the
  dossier's note. If a portrait exists it is elsewhere (a Boston-area
  notice, perhaps). Listed in the attach script for when one surfaces.
- **Michael Giocondo, Robert Good, Joan Reilly, Eugene Dixon** — the
  documentary's participant-update photographs exist but every online
  copy is a 60×60 or 111×111 thumbnail (camden28.org and the PBS POV
  pages, live and archived). The full-size images would have to come
  from the filmmaker.
- **Jayma Abdoo** — photographs are in her archival collection and not
  digitized.
- **Rosemary Reilly** — the FBI surveillance photograph shows her with
  Ridolfi carrying bags before the raid; it is an identification
  photograph, not a portrait, and no adequate reproduction was located.
