# Piety Street defendants — photo provenance

Source: a curator-supplied composite sheet
(`piety_street_panthers_verified_photos29.png`, delivered via a LimeWire
share, 794x2048), assembling eight halftone newspaper portraits of the
New Orleans Piety Street defendants. The portraits are period newsprint
cuts — visibly from The Black Panther newspaper's contemporaneous
coverage of the case (newsprint body text is legible at several crop
edges) — and **each portrait carries its own printed caption**, which is
the identification anchor for every crop below. Crops were taken from
the sheet, sized to the site's 525x700 convention; the printed captions
were cropped out of the final images but are preserved in the source
sheet retained by the curator.
