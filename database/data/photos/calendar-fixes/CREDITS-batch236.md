## Deliberately not filled

- **Bloody Sunday, 7 March 1965.** The free Library of Congress photographs
  of Selma are Peter Pettus's pictures of the successful march of 21–25
  March — marchers with flags, in daylight, unmolested. Using one for the
  day people were beaten on the Edmund Pettus Bridge would misrepresent it
  in the same way the Soledad Brothers picket misrepresented the Marin
  County courthouse.
- **Greensboro sit-ins, 1 February 1960.** The free image available is Carol
  Highsmith's 2017 photograph of the preserved Woolworth counter — a museum
  piece standing in for the event, which is the fault already corrected on
  the Frances Hart record.
- **Bodies of Chaney, Goodman and Schwerner recovered, 4 August 1964.** The
  obvious free image is the FBI missing poster, and this calendar already
  uses it, on 22 June, for the day they disappeared. One picture on two
  entries is worse than one entry without.
