Priscilla Grim is a writer, organizer, and long-time movement media-maker born in Atlanta and based in Brooklyn. A co-founder of the We Are the 99 Percent Tumblr that helped spark Occupy Wall Street, she has spent more than a decade building independent media in support of grassroots movements.

In 2023 she traveled to Atlanta to join the encampment defending the Weelaunee Forest and the movement to Stop Cop City. She was arrested in the forest and held for 31 days in DeKalb County Jail. Her essay on that experience, “31 Days in DeKalb County Hell,” was published by Scalawag Magazine.
