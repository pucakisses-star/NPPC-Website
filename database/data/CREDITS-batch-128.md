# Batch 128 — Josephine Sunshine Overaker portrait credit and provenance

| Slug | Depicts | Source | Provenance |
|------|---------|--------|------------|
| josephine-sunshine-overaker | Josephine Sunshine Overaker, Operation Backfire (ELF/ALF) fugitive | FBI wanted poster, `fbi.gov/wanted/dt/josephine-sunshine-overaker/download.pdf`, Portland Field Office | Public domain (US federal government work) |

**Identity.** The poster names her throughout and is issued by the FBI
field office running the case. No two-fact problem arises: the image
comes from the wanted notice itself.

**Which image.** The poster carries four pictures. This is the
photograph the notice leads with — an identity-document-style portrait
against a blue ground, the largest and sharpest of the set at 513×686.
Deliberately **not** used:

- the *"Photograph Age Progressed to 41 Years Old"* rendering, which is
  a forensic artist's projection rather than a photograph of her, and
  would misrepresent the record as showing what she looks like;
- the *"Tattoo of bird on back"* photograph, which shows her back and
  identifies her by a distinguishing mark rather than portraying her;
- a second, softer portrait (450×598) superseded by the one used.

**Crop.** 513×686 is already 3:4 to within half a percent, so the image
was resampled straight to the standard 525×700 panel — a 1.02× change,
no cropping and no meaningful resampling loss.
