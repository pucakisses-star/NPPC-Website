# Batch 125 — Bill Sutherland portrait credit and provenance

One photograph, supplied by the curator, cropped to the standard
525×700 panel and attached into an empty photo slot.

| Slug | Depicts | Source | Provenance |
|------|---------|--------|------------|
| bill-sutherland | Bill Sutherland (1918–2010), WWII draft resister and War Resisters League field secretary for Africa | War Resisters League, `warresisters.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/billsutherlandsmile_0.jpg` | Organisational photo published by the WRL, the organisation he worked for; supplied by the curator |

**Identity.** The filename names him, and the WRL is the organisation
he served as Africa representative for decades — this is the portrait
that organisation publishes of him. The photograph is undated; the
embedded EXIF records only a January 2010 scan, the month he died, so
it is very likely a file image reissued for the obituary rather than a
2010 photograph.

**Crop.** The source is 163×245 and is the only resolution the WRL
publishes. It was cropped to the 3:4 panel ratio from the top — the
bottom strip of shirt was dropped, no part of the head or face — then
resampled to 525×700 to match the collection standard. It is a 3.2×
upscale and will look soft next to a natively large portrait; that is
the source, not the processing. If a higher-resolution original
surfaces (the WRL archive at Swarthmore's Peace Collection is the
obvious place), it should overwrite the same
`prisoners/bill-sutherland.jpg` path.
