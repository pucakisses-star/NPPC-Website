# DOB audit — deceased records with year-only birthdates (batch 100)

A ten-agent research sweep over all 253 deceased records whose birthdates
were stored at year (or month) precision. Every CONFIRMED date required a
citable source whose subject matched the record on at least two facts
beyond the name (death date, case story, location); people-search/data-
broker sites were forbidden. 65 confirmed, 174 not found, 14 conflicts.

## Not found

The remaining 174 records have no citable exact birth date. For most, the
year-only state is the true limit of the historical record: people born
enslaved, Native leaders whose births predate written registration, WWI-era
deportees known only from Kenyon Zimmer's index (which records years only),
and suffragists whose Alexander Street dictionary entries carry years only.
Leads worth revisiting: james-b-mcnamara (Find a Grave June 2 1882, fetch
blocked), edward-murphy (NYT Legacy 2012 notice, blocked), edna-mary-purtell
(month February 1899 available), willie-mcgee (SNAC 1915-11-04, but the
scholarship deliberately treats his birth as uncertain).

The living-records tranche (~576, mostly recent protest arrestees) was
deliberately excluded: their DOBs circulate only on people-search sites,
which fail both the sourcing and the ethics bar. The FBI-listed fugitives
among them, whose posters publish DOBs officially, are the one targetable
subset if wanted.
