**Identity — weaker than most entries here, and worth saying so.**
The URL is an opaque CDN asset with no caption, headline or byline
attached, and no page carrying this exact image was found. The
identification rests on the curator supplying it, plus consistency: it
is a Swedish press portrait of a man in his late forties, which is
what Hemler was when he came forward in Uppsala in June 2012 aged 49,
and Bonnier News is a Swedish newspaper group that would plausibly
have photographed him then.

That is *consistency*, not the two-fact standard the batch 110 credits
describe. Compare the Overaker portrait in batch 128, where the source
document names the subject on its face. The Swedish coverage that was
findable — Upsala Nya Tidning, whose December 21, 2013 piece covers
the Tax Agency refusing his application to reclaim his original
identity — illustrates the photograph with a US Air Force phantom
sketch rather than this portrait, so it does not corroborate the
image.

If a captioned publication of this photograph turns up, or a different
portrait with a byline, it should overwrite the same
`prisoners/david-hemler.jpg` path.

**Rights.** A press photograph on a commercial newspaper CDN, used
here to illustrate the subject's own biographical record. No licence
statement accompanies the asset. This is a weaker rights position than
the public-domain federal material used elsewhere in the collection
(the FBI posters in batches 128 and 131); if the collection needs a
clean licence for every image, this one should be replaced.

**Crop.** The source is landscape, 683×430. The 3:4 panel was taken
from x=51 to x=373 — centred on the head rather than on the frame,
since he sits well left of centre — at full height, then resampled to
the standard 525×700. That is a 1.63× upscale.
